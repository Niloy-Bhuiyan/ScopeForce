# Product

ScopeForce lets product and engineering teams control AI-assisted software delivery without reducing the work to a chat transcript. Its durable object is the Engineering Graph: a traceable chain from intent to evidence. The product is useful when teams need to explain why code exists, what a changed requirement affects, which work can run safely in parallel, and why a result is considered complete.

The MVP focuses on one coherent CampusLink project. Its product center is REQ-014, email verification, traced through ADR-007, TASK-021, `auth/verify.ts`, TEST-014, and RESULT-014.
